# Paper Maker Official
### Smarter Papers. Better Assessments.
**Version 1.0.0 — Initial Release**

Create, customize, and manage school examination papers in one place.

## Features
- **School Branding:** Personalize your dashboard and paper header with your school name, logo, and colour scheme.
- **Syllabus-Based Papers:** Upload syllabus files, select chapters or the complete document, and generate papers with Strict Syllabus Lock.
- **Handwriting & Image Import:** Convert handwritten or photographed questions into editable paper content.
- **Flexible Question Editing:** Add, edit, and remove questions; select question types, quantities, and optional answer lines.
- **Urdu & English Support:** Create papers with language-appropriate text direction.
- **Paper Preview & PDF Export:** Review your paper layout and download a PDF.
- **Saved Papers:** Save and organize papers for future use.
- **Account & Profile:** Register, sign in, manage your profile, verify your email, and reset your password.

## Getting Started
1. Download the Windows installer from the official Releases page and install Paper Maker Official.
2. Open the app and create an account or sign in. Complete email verification when prompted.
3. Add your school details and logo in School Settings.
4. Follow the Gemini API key instructions on the **How to use it** page to enable AI features.
5. Upload your syllabus or question images, choose the required question types, and create your paper.
6. Review and edit the questions, check the PDF preview, then save or download your paper.

## Internet & AI Features
Internet is required for account authentication, verification and password-reset emails, Gemini-powered features, and update downloads. Gemini requires an API key and is subject to the provider's usage limits. Review imported text and generated questions before printing, especially when using handwritten or scanned pages.

## App Updates
When a newer published version is available, the installed app offers **Update now** or **Later**. After downloading, select **Restart & Install** when you are ready. You can also use **Help → Check for updates**. Save your current work before restarting.
